EduSYS

EduSYS is our school management platform. A school's administration is the same work every term: admissions, fees, attendance, results, keeping parents informed. EduSYS puts that work in one place and automates what repeats, so that a school can give its attention to teaching.

Each school runs on its own web address, with its own data, staff and branding.

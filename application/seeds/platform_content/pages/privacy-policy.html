How HyperCom handles the information behind EduSYS: the school's account, the records a school keeps about its students, parents and staff, the payments made through it, and the messages it sends.

1. Who we are

EduSYS is a school management platform operated by HyperCom (“HyperCom”, “we”, “us”), a Nigerian information technology company with its head office at 14 Akinremi Street, Ikeja, Lagos.

HyperCom is the data controller for the information about schools that register with us and their owners and contacts, our billing records, and visitors to edusys.ng. For the records a school keeps in EduSYS about its students, parents, guardians, applicants and staff, the school is the data controller and HyperCom acts as a data processor on the school's instructions.

This policy covers the edusys.ng website, each school's EduSYS address (for example yourschool.edusys.ng), the EduSYS applications, and the email and SMS messages EduSYS sends. It does not cover websites or services we link to.

2. Information we collect

School and owner information

The school's name, address, web address and branding, and the name, email address and telephone number of the owner and the people who manage the school's subscription.

Records a school keeps

What a school chooses to record about its students and applicants: names, dates of birth, photographs, classes, guardians and their contact details, admission forms and documents, attendance, behaviour records, homework, examination marks, results and report cards, fees and payments, and library, transport and hostel records. About staff: names, contact details, roles, leave, attendance and payroll details.

Payment information

Subscription plan, number of students, invoices and payment status for schools; fee payments and admission form fees for parents and applicants. Card and bank details are entered with our payment providers (Paystack, Flutterwave, Monnify, Interswitch or Remita) and are not stored by EduSYS. For bank transfers we keep the receipt that is uploaded and the details entered with it.

Usage and technical data

Sign-in times, actions taken in EduSYS, IP address, browser and device type, and error logs. We use this to keep the platform secure and to help when something goes wrong.

Communications

Support requests, enquiries and quote requests, the files sent with them, and our replies.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the service: run each school's records, admissions, fees, results, messages and website as the school directs.
  • To administer accounts: set up schools and users, apply roles and permissions, and manage subscriptions.
  • To process payments: record subscription payments, fee payments and admission form fees, and confirm bank transfers.
  • To keep the platform secure: separate each school's data, detect misuse and investigate incidents.
  • To support schools: diagnose problems and correct records at the school's request.
  • To improve the service: understand which features are used and fix problems, using aggregated or de-identified data where possible.
  • To meet our obligations: keep accounting and tax records, and respond to lawful requests.
  • To communicate: service notices, security notifications and changes that affect a school.

4. Our legal bases

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 we rely on the following bases, depending on the activity:

  • Performance of our contract with the school: operating EduSYS and providing support.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the platform and its data secure, preventing misuse and improving the service, balanced against the rights of the people concerned.
  • Legal obligation: accounting, tax and responding to valid requests from regulators or courts.
  • Consent, where it is required, for example optional messages.

For the records a school keeps, the school decides its own lawful basis, as the controller.

5. Children's information

Many students are under 18. Their information is entered and controlled by their school, which is responsible for informing parents and guardians and for any consent the law requires. We process it only to provide EduSYS to that school, never for advertising, and we do not sell it or use it to build profiles.

6. Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in, protect your session and remember your choices, such as accepting cookies on a school website. These cannot be switched off without breaking the service. We do not use advertising cookies or sell data to advertising networks.

7. Who we share information with

We share personal data only as needed to run the service, and only with parties bound by confidentiality and data-protection obligations:

  • Hosting providers that store the platform and its data.
  • Email and SMS providers that deliver the messages a school or EduSYS sends.
  • Payment providers, for subscription payments, fee payments and admission form fees.
  • Video providers such as Zoom or Google Meet, where a school uses those add-ons.
  • Professional advisers, auditors and insurers, under confidentiality.
  • Authorities, where we are required by law or where disclosure is necessary to investigate misuse of the platform.
  • A successor entity, in the event of a merger, acquisition or transfer of the business.

A school's information is never visible to another school.

8. Where data is stored

EduSYS is hosted on cloud servers, and some providers may process data outside Nigeria. Where data leaves Nigeria we rely on contractual safeguards, access controls and the transfer conditions permitted by the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

We do not sell personal data, and we never share a school's records with third parties for their own marketing.

9. How long we keep information

  • A school's records: for as long as the school's subscription is active. After it ends, we keep them for up to 90 days so the school can renew or ask for a copy, then delete them.
  • Records a school deletes: removed from the platform when the school deletes them, and from backups as the backups are replaced.
  • Billing and accounting records: for the period required by Nigerian tax and company law.
  • Support correspondence: normally up to 24 months after the request is closed.
  • Information that must be kept longer for a legal claim, or that we are required by law to retain, is kept only for that purpose.

10. How we protect information

Data is encrypted in transit. Each school's data is kept separate, and each user sees only what their role allows. Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes, and two-factor sign-in is available. Administrative access to the platform is limited to staff who need it.

No system is completely secure. If you believe an account has been compromised, contact the school, or us at info@hypercom.net.ng, immediately.

11. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask for access to your personal data, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, restrict or object to certain processing, ask for a portable copy, or withdraw consent you gave.

  • If you are a student, parent, guardian, applicant or member of staff, the school controls your records: please contact the school first. We will refer requests about a school's records to that school and help it respond.
  • Other requests can be sent to info@hypercom.net.ng. We verify identity before acting and respond within 30 days.
  • You may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) if you believe your data has not been handled lawfully.

12. Responsibilities of schools

A school using EduSYS decides what it records and why. The school is responsible for having a lawful basis for its records, for informing students, parents, guardians and staff how their information is used, for setting roles and permissions appropriately, and for keeping its list of users current.

13. Changes to this policy

We review this policy as the service and the law change. The date at the top shows when it last changed. We tell schools about material changes before they take effect; using EduSYS after that date means the updated policy applies.
